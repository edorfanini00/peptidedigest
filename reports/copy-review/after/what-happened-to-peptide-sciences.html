What Happened to Peptide Sciences?

March 10, 2026·Updated September 25, 2026·The Peptide Digest Staff

Peptide Sciences says it has voluntarily shut down operations and discontinued the sale of its research products. That is the explanation in the company's published shutdown notice. The notice does not say the DOJ closed the business.

The notice remains more useful than a guessed reason for the closure. It confirms the end of research-product sales but does not explain the company's internal decision or resolve the status of each customer's order.

What Peptide Sciences has said

The company says it retains the rights to its name and trademarks. It also warns that third parties claiming to be affiliated with Peptide Sciences, to be its successor or to sell its products are not authorized. A familiar logo on another site is not evidence of continuity.

The notice does not give a reopening date. An earlier version of this article claimed thousands of customers received no refunds or had orders go unfulfilled; that specific claim went beyond what the notice and available evidence established, and we have removed it.

Was the shutdown caused by FDA or DOJ action?

The word "voluntary" is the company's description. It does not reveal whether legal advice, regulation or business conditions affected the decision. We have not established a government order requiring this closure and should not present one as the cause.

The FDA has challenged research-use disclaimers at other sellers. Its March 31, 2026 warning letter to Gram Peptides explains how product claims can show intended human use. That is regulatory context, not evidence that the FDA or DOJ directed the Peptide Sciences shutdown.
